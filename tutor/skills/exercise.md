---
name: exercise
description: A hands-on exercise sized to where you stand, on this lesson or a topic you name
argument-hint: "[topic]"
---
Set the learner one hands-on exercise, on the topic they named, or else on what this lesson covers now.
- Size it to where they stand (read `notes.md` and the conversation): one step past what they did on their own.
- Put its files in the lesson's folder, and define it with `exercise_define`: its steps, and a check command that passes only when their own work is right.
- Never solve it for them, and never write their files. Point them at the files to open and the tab to use.
- Note the exercise in progress in `notes.md`.
