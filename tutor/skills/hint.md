Give the learner the next hint on the exercise in progress: one step further than the last hint you gave (`notes.md` lists them), never the solution.
- Look at their files first, to hint at where they are actually stuck.
- With no exercise in progress, ask them what they are stuck on.
- Add the hint you gave to `notes.md`, under the exercise.
