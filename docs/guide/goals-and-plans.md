# Goals and the study plan

A project opens on **Goals**, the first entry in its list of lessons. It is a conversation in which the tutor asks what you want to be able to do, what you know already and how much time you have: a few questions. Then it proposes a study plan, which you edit and approve; the lessons it holds appear under Goals in the sidebar.

## The plan card

The tutor's proposal is a card in the conversation, **Proposed study plan**: modules, each a list of lessons with a title and an objective.

Change anything on the card before you decide: edit titles and objectives, move lessons up and down, remove a lesson or a module, **+ Add a lesson**, **+ Add a module**. Then:

| Press | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Approve** | The modules and lessons appear in the list under Goals, as you left them on the card. |
| **Dismiss** | Nothing changes. |

Nothing changes until you approve. The tutor learns what you decided with your next message: you do not need to tell it. A card that waits for you is marked with a dot (●) beside its lesson in the sidebar.

## Changes to the plan

Later, a lesson's tutor can propose a **change to the plan** the same way: a lesson added to go over something again, another order, a lesson dropped. It is a card too, **Proposed change to the plan**, which marks new lessons as **new** and lists those that **Would be dropped**; **Keep this lesson in the plan** takes one out of the drop. Edit it, then approve or dismiss it.

## The list of lessons

Each lesson in the sidebar shows its state: ○ not started, ▸ in progress, ✓ done, × dropped. A pulsing mark means its tutor is working right now. Hover over a lesson to read its objective. Lessons can be taken in any order: click one to open it (see [Lessons](lessons.md)).

| To | Do |
|---|---|
| Add a lesson by hand, outside the plan | **+ New lesson** at the end of the list: a title and an optional objective. |
| Rename a lesson or change its objective | The **⋯** beside it, then **Save**. |
| Delete a lesson and its conversation | The **⋯** beside it, then **Delete**. The files the tutor made in the sandbox stay. |
| Show or hide a project's lessons | The ▸ / ▾ beside the project. |

Goals keeps its name and its place: it has no ⋯.
